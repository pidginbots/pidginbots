How it works

Three steps, fully automated

The engine runs on a schedule so your pipeline refills itself every single morning, whether you open a laptop or not.

01 / LISTEN

Scan the live web

Every morning at 7am the engine sweeps public platforms for the exact phrases people use when they need a product like yours.

02 / SCORE

Rank buying intent

Every match is scored on urgency, specificity and fit. Only genuine buyer signals survive, noise is filtered out.

03 / DELIVER

Get your digest

You receive a ranked digest with a suggested opening message for each lead. Copy, send, close. Done.
