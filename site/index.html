Capabilities

Built to find buyers, not followers.

Every feature exists for one purpose: putting your product in front of people who are already looking for it.

Signal Detection

The engine watches public posts, forums, and communities across the web for buying signals — direct requests, frustration with competitors, and demand spikes related to your product.

Intelligent Lead Scoring

Not every mention matters. Each lead is scored on intent strength, urgency, and reachability, so you always see who is worth contacting first — and who can wait.

Daily Digest + Openers

Every morning you receive a clean digest of fresh leads — each with the original post, why it matched your product, and a suggested opening message you can send in one click.

Unlimited Products

Track one product or an entire portfolio. Each product gets its own keyword set, its own buyer profile, and its own lead pipeline.

Zero Leaks

Your product profiles and lead pipelines stay private to your account. Competitors never see what you are tracking.

Always On

The engine does not take evenings, weekends, or holidays off. Buyers announce needs at 2am; PidginBots is already listening.
